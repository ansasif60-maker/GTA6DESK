The State of Leonida: Atlas, Districts & Lore

From the neon-drenched coastal avenues of Vice City to the untamed swamps of Grassrivers and the secluded coral atolls of the Keys, examine Rockstar’s most ambitious open world to date.

Cartographic Overview: State of Leonida encompasses over 120 square kilometers of terrestrial terrain, inland waterways, and maritime shallows. Open Interactive Map Explorer →
REGIONAL DOSSIERS

Major Districts & Metropolitan Hubs

METROPOLITAN CAPITAL SECTOR 01

Vice City Metropolitan Area

The cultural and economic epicenter of Leonida. Inspired by modern Miami, Vice City features towering high-rise financial districts, luxury waterfront estates on Starfish Island, vibrant nightlife on Ocean Beach, and culturally rich enclave neighborhoods like Little Haiti.

  • Key Landmarks: Ocean Drive art deco strip, Vice City Port, Malibu Club, Starfish Island mansions.
  • Atmospheric Traits: High-density NPC crowds, supercars, luxury yachts, relentless beach culture.
  • Law Enforcement Density: Extreme (VCPD quick-reaction units, aerial tactical helicopters).
COMMERCIAL PORT SECTOR 02

Port Gellhorn & Industrial Corridor

A sprawling, grittier shipping and logistics port situated along Leonida’s western coast. Port Gellhorn serves as the backbone of Leonida’s industrial cargo operations, container terminals, automotive rail depots, and illicit black-market supply lines.

  • Key Landmarks: Container shipping basin, heavy cargo freight yards, motel row, local racetrack.
  • Atmospheric Traits: Heavy freight trucks, industrial smoke, working-class diners, chop shops.
  • Criminal Activity: Smuggling manifests, armed depot robberies, longshoreman syndicate operations.
WETLANDS & MARSH SECTOR 03

The Grassrivers (Leonida Everglades)

An expansive, treacherous subtropical wetland expanse dominating central and southern Leonida. Dense sawgrass, mangrove canopies, and murky bayous make land navigation hazardous, requiring specialized fan-powered airboats and 4x4 off-road rigs.

  • Key Landmarks: Airboat tour docks, sunken smuggling aircraft wrecks, isolated poacher cabins.
  • Fauna AI: Aggressive American alligators, predatory wild boars, flamingos, black bears.
  • Tactical Utility: Ideal secluded drop zones for evading police cordons and hiding illicit contraband.
ARCHIPELAGO SECTOR 04

The Leonida Keys & Overseas Highway

A chain of picturesque limestone and coral islands stretching off the southern tip of the mainland, linked by a massive multi-span overseas highway. Famous for sport fishing, luxury beachfront villas, shallow sandbars, and clandestine maritime smuggling.

  • Key Landmarks: Seven-Mile Bridge equivalent, marina resorts, tiki bars, lighthouse outposts.
  • Atmospheric Traits: Pristine turquoise waters, leisure speedboats, open horizons, tropical sunsets.
  • Gameplay Dynamic: High-speed bridge chases and deep-water boat escapes into international waters.
