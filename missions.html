GTA 6 Missions: Details, Walkthroughs & Payouts

Complete chronological guide to Grand Theft Auto VI's campaign missions and emergent heists. Featuring high-resolution mission screenshots, cash payouts, gold medal 100% completion requirements, and tactical partner coordination tips.

1. Bad Luck, I Guess (Prologue)

$0 (Parole Release)
Protagonist: Lucia · District: Leonida State Penitentiary · Difficulty: Story Prologue (1/10)

Lucia’s correctional interview with Stefanie inside the state penitentiary, followed by her parole processing, mandatory ankle monitor fitting, and emotional reunion with Jason outside the prison gates. Introduces conversational dialogue branches that define early relationship chemistry.

Gold Medal 100% Checklist
  • ✓ Time Trial: Complete parole departure within 06:30.
  • ✓ Composure: Zero hesitation during Stefanie’s interrogation prompts.
  • ✓ Contraband: Successfully conceal contraband shiv past gate metal detector.
TACTICAL BRIEFING: Pay close attention to dialogue choices. Being cooperative reduces initial parole officer check-in frequencies, unlocking greater free-roam radius before trigger events.

2. Trust & Firepower (Convenience Holdup)

$3,450 + Contraband Goods
Protagonists: Lucia & Jason (Dual Switch) · District: Kelly County / North Suburbs · Difficulty: Early Heist (3/10)

Lucia and Jason pull off a synchronized late-night holdup of a rural 24/7 store. Lucia intimidates the clerk and keeps panic levels manageable, while Jason clears the cash register and drills into the manager's drop safe before police squad cars arrive.

Gold Medal 100% Checklist
  • ✓ Fast Hands: Clean out safe and register within 90 seconds.
  • ✓ Zero Alarms: Subdue customer attempting to dial 911 on smartphone.
  • ✓ Clean Getaway: Lose Kelly County sheriff cruiser without bodywork damage.
TACTICAL BRIEFING: Use the Contextual Partner Wheel to command Jason to bag cash while holding your aim on the clerk. Shoot the ceiling once if the clerk's intimidation meter drops into red.

3. The Bank of Leonida Vault Breach

$1,850,000 (Major Score)
Protagonists: Lucia & Jason · District: Downtown Vice City Financial Core · Difficulty: Major Score Heist (8/10)

A high-stakes tactical heist targeting the subterranean safe deposit depository of the Bank of Leonida. Features high-temperature thermite vault drilling, hostage containment in the grand lobby, and a heavy SWAT firefight through the subway tunnels under Brickell.

Gold Medal 100% Checklist
  • ✓ Precision Thermal: Burn through vault locks without letting drill overheat.
  • ✓ Discipline: Complete bank clearance with zero civilian casualties.
  • ✓ Rapid Transit: Lose 4-star VCPD SWAT pursuit in subway tracks under 3 minutes.
TACTICAL BRIEFING: Pre-stow the Tactical Service Carbine MK II in the trunk of your getaway vehicle before starting. Sidearms alone will not penetrate SWAT ballistic shields.

4. Keys Midnight Contraband Intercept

$420,000 + Seized Narcotics
Protagonists: Jason & Lucia · District: The Vice Keys & International Waters · Difficulty: Maritime Combat (6/10)

Hijacking a cartel contraband shipment across the choppy Florida Straits using Shitzu Tropic Offshore speedboats while dodging Coast Guard radar helicopters. Features dynamic ocean wave physics and synchronized drive-by fire.

Gold Medal 100% Checklist
  • ✓ Boarding Party: Leap onto the lead cartel speedboat while at 50+ knots.
  • ✓ Rotor Snip: Shoot down pursuit helicopter tail rotor using rifle fire.
  • ✓ Dock Safe: Deliver contraband cargo to Cal Hampton's marina before dawn.
TACTICAL BRIEFING: Take advantage of wave crests. Aiming when your boat hits the peak of an ocean swell provides a steady 1-second firing window before landing in the trough.

5. High Rollerz Nightclub Infiltration

$125,000 + VIP Black Book
Protagonist: Lucia · District: Vice Beach / Ocean Drive · Difficulty: Stealth & Social Infiltration (5/10)

Lucia dresses up to infiltrate a VIP rooftop party at The Malibu Club on Ocean Drive. Her objective is to clone the encrypted smartphone keycards of a corrupt venture capitalist while strictly avoiding tourists and clubgoers streaming live video on their phones.

Gold Medal 100% Checklist
  • ✓ Ghost Infiltrator: Remain completely undetected by VIP private security.
  • ✓ No Viral Footprint: Avoid appearing in any tourist livestream camera cones.
  • ✓ Cyber Breach: Complete NFC proximity clone in under 45 seconds.
TACTICAL BRIEFING: Look for yellow phone recording cones on your radar. If an influencer points their smartphone camera at Lucia, turn around and blend into the bar queue immediately.
