Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Last Updated: September 2026. Review how GTA 6 Desk respects user privacy, complies with global data frameworks, and minimizes digital footprint.

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

At GTA 6 Desk (accessible from gta6desk.com), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is received and collected by GTA 6 Desk and how it is used. We operate under a strict principle of data minimization—we collect only what is strictly necessary to deliver fast, secure editorial content.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two main categories:

  • Non-Personally Identifiable Telemetry: Standard web server log data, including internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, operating system, date/time stamp, and clickstream data. This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual visitors.
  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you send a communication through our Contact & Corrections form or email our editorial desk, we receive your name, email address, and message contents. This information is used exclusively to respond to your inquiry and is never sold or shared.

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

GTA 6 Desk does not use invasive cross-site tracking cookies. We utilize standard HTTP session tokens and modern browser localStorage strictly for user experience preferences:

  • UI State Storage: Remembering your calculator scenario presets or map filters during your browsing session.
  • Zero Third-Party Data Harvesting: We do not sell your behavioral browsing data to third-party data brokers or predatory ad retargeting networks.

4. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under Regulation (EU) 2016/679:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are afforded specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected over the past 12 months.
  • Right to Opt-Out: We do not sell personal information. However, you maintain the statutory right to confirm non-sale of data.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you in pricing or access for exercising any privacy rights.

6. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures—including TLS 1.3 encryption, HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), and Content Security Policies—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, or disclosure.

7. Data Protection Officer & Inquiries

For privacy-related requests or to exercise your statutory data rights, please contact our Data Governance team at privacy@gta6desk.com.
