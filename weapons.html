Realistic Ballistics & Trunk Stowage

Rockstar Games has abandoned the bottomless weapon wheel from GTA V. In GTA 6, players carry a limited on-person loadout (concealed sidearm, shoulder sling, or duffel bag). Heavy ordnance like assault rifles and rocket launchers must be stowed inside your vehicle's trunk.
