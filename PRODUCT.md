# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Multi-page static semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS design system, modular vanilla JavaScript for interactive tools, and Next.js 15 App Router codebase for edge deployment. Zero complex client-side JS traps for 100% crawlable Answer Engine Optimization (AEO).

## Users

1. **GTA 6 Enthusiasts & Gamers:** Seeking verified news, trailer analyses, release updates for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, world lore (Leonida/Vice City), and gameplay guides.
2. **Business & Financial Readers / Industry Observers:** Tracking Take-Two Interactive (NASDAQ: TTWO) corporate earnings, Net Bookings guidance, commercial projections, and the economic impact of the biggest entertainment release in history.
3. **AI Search Engines & Answer Engines (AEO/GEO/LLMO):** Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google Search crawlers seeking clean, crawlable HTML articles with structured semantic markup, explicit facts, and zero client-side hydration barriers.

## Product Purpose

GTA 6 Desk (`gta6desk.com`) is a premium editorial gaming and business journal dedicated to Grand Theft Auto VI. It combines high-quality gaming journalism, deep world lore, and comprehensive gameplay guides with an authoritative focus on the business, financial metrics, and corporate performance of Take-Two Interactive.

## Positioning

Unlike clickbait rumor mills or ad-choked wikis, GTA 6 Desk provides calm, authoritative, verified gaming journalism paired with Wall Street-level industry analysis. It clearly distinguishes verified corporate disclosures (SEC filings, Rockstar Newswire) from editorial speculation, and offers dedicated, user-friendly interactive tools.

## Operating Context

- Readers visit from desktop and mobile seeking clean, fast-loading, easily readable editorial content.
- Platform exclusivity is respected: GTA 6 is launching on **PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S** in Fall 2025.
- Layouts are user-friendly, scan-friendly, and categorized by topic with dedicated subpages for deep dives.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Multi-Page Architecture:** Dedicated pages for Home, Financial Desk, News, Leonida & World, Guides, Interactive Tools (ROI Calculator, Map Explorer, Vehicle Database), and complete Legal/Compliance suite (About, Privacy Policy, Terms, Disclaimer, Contact).
- **Live Launch Countdown Engine:** Real-time multi-target countdown console tracking target launch dates and elapsed milestone time with millisecond precision and .ics calendar synchronization.
- **Official 4K Trailer Theater & Scene Intel:** 16:9 responsive cinema player for official Rockstar Games trailers with interactive frame-by-frame scene seeking, camera telemetry, and ambient theater mode.
- **Fact vs. Rumor Radar & Community Hype Poll:** Filterable intelligence matrix separating verified disclosures from leaks and debunked rumors, paired with real-time community protagonist voting.
- **Full Static HTML Visibility (AEO):** Every article, headline, and data summary is rendered in clean static HTML for instant indexation by search and LLM engines.
- **Data Transparency:** Clear distinction between verified SEC filings / analyst consensus and analytical scenario projection models. No misleading claims of live unverified broker APIs.
- **Monetization & Compliance:** Fixed zero-CLS ad reservations, IAB TCF 2.2 privacy compliance, and strict Fair Use & Trademark disclaimers.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone:** Premium gaming and financial journal — authoritative, sleek, polished, and reader-first.
- **Visual Character:** Sophisticated dark editorial magazine with subtle Vice City magenta and cyan accents, clean cards, and generous whitespace.
- **Integrity:** Independent journalistic publication not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
