Lucia

Voice: Manni L. Perez
Leonida Dept of Corrections / Independent Duo
Origin / Base
Leonida Penitentiary / Vice City
Specialty
Tactical CQB & Intimidation

The groundbreaking first female playable protagonist in Rockstar's modern 3D era. Hardened by incarceration in Leonida State Penitentiary, Lucia is a calculated, streetwise tactician whose bond with Jason anchors the narrative's high-stakes Bonnie-and-Clyde robberies.

Calculated Criminal Mind High Combat Reflexes Fast Lockpicking Crowd Intimidation

SYSTEM MECHANICS

Dual-Operative Synchronized AI & Switching

Unlike previous GTA titles where protagonists operate in distant silos, Lucia and Jason can be switched seamlessly mid-combat, during high-speed police chases, or while executing synchronized bank robberies.

  • Contextual Partner Wheel: Direct your partner to bag cash, watch the rear door, or breach security locks.
  • Vehicle Trunk Armory Sharing: Transfer heavy carbines and breaching shotguns between characters via vehicle trunks.
  • Autonomous Pursuit Evasion: When you switch to the shooter, your partner takes the wheel with high-tier evasive driving AI.
