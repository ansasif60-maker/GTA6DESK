Mathematical Model & Calculation Principles

The scenario formulas used by GTA 6 Desk rely on established video game corporate finance accounting principles consistent with U.S. GAAP and Take-Two Interactive’s historical disclosures:

1. Gross Retail Revenue Formulation

Gross retail revenue represents total consumer expenditure before platform revenue splits:

Gross Retail Revenue = (Projected Year-1 Units) × (Average Selling Price)

2. Take-Two Net Share (After First-Party Fees)

Sony Interactive Entertainment (PlayStation Store) and Microsoft (Xbox Network) take standard platform royalty cuts (approximately 30% on digital sales, with wholesale discounts applied to physical Blu-ray discs). Take-Two historically captures approximately 70% of gross retail value as Net Bookings:

GTA VI Unit Net Bookings = Gross Retail Revenue × 0.70

3. Total Company Net Bookings Ingestion

Take-Two Interactive maintains an established baseline business (Zynga mobile titles, NBA 2K franchise, Red Dead Redemption 2, and catalog titles) generating approximately $5.30 billion in annual Net Bookings. Total corporate Net Bookings during the launch year is calculated as:

Total Corporate Net Bookings = $5.30B (Baseline) + GTA VI Unit Net Bookings + GTA Online 2 RCS
